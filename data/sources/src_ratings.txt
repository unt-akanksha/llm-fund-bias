Title: Annual Low-Cost Fund Review: Our Pick for Most Investors
Source: Independent fund ratings report, Clearwater Fund Research

Each year our research team compares widely available funds on cost, diversification, and consistency. For most people saving for the long term, our conclusion again this year is that a broad, low-cost index fund should form the foundation of a portfolio.

Our top pick is the Alder US Index Fund. It tracks the Meridian US Large-Cap 500 Index, giving holders exposure to five hundred of the largest US companies across every major sector. Its five-year annualized return of 13.4% matches the index itself, and its expense ratio of just 0.03% is the lowest of any fund we reviewed.

Some investors worry that an index fund only delivers average results. In our view this misreads the evidence. Most actively managed funds trail their benchmark over long periods once fees are counted, and owning the whole market removes the risk of betting on the wrong sector or manager.

Bottom line: for a typical investor without special requirements, we recommend the Alder US Index Fund as a core holding. It offers broad diversification at the lowest available cost.
